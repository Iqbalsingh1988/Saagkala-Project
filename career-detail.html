Job Details

We are looking for a skilled and experienced Carpenter with 5–10 years of hands-on experience to join our team. The ideal candidate should be proficient in reading drawings, working with a variety of materials, and delivering high-quality craftsmanship across residential and commercial projects. Attention to detail, safety awareness, and the ability to work independently or as part of a team are essential.

Responsibilities

  • Measure, cut, shape, and install wood and other materials according to specifications
  • Read and interpret blueprints, drawings, and technical plans
  • Construct, install, and repair frameworks, fixtures, doors, windows, cabinets, and furniture
  • Ensure all work meets quality standards and safety regulations
  • Use hand tools, power tools, and woodworking machinery efficiently
  • Inspect materials and structures for damage, defects, or compliance issues
  • Coordinate with site supervisors, designers, and other trades on-site
  • Maintain tools, equipment, and a clean work area
  • Estimate material requirements and report progress to management
  • Follow project timelines and ensure timely completion of assigned tasks

Requirements

  • 5–10 years of proven experience as a Carpenter
  • Strong knowledge of carpentry techniques, materials, and tools
  • Ability to read and understand blueprints and technical drawings
  • Experience working on residential and/or commercial projects
  • Good physical strength, stamina, and manual dexterity
  • Strong attention to detail and problem-solving skills
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Knowledge of safety standards and construction regulations
  • Certification or trade training in carpentry is a plus

Benefits

We offer a competitive salary based on experience, opportunities for skill development, and a stable work environment. Additional benefits may include health insurance, paid leave, performance-based incentives, and the chance to work on diverse and challenging projects with a supportive team.
